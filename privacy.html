Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

SimpleBrowsing — Focus Reader & Recipe Cleaner is designed to respect your privacy. This policy explains in plain English how the browser extension handles data.

1. Local-Only Webpage Processing

SimpleBrowsing only runs when you explicitly click the extension's toolbar icon. It reads the active webpage's text and structured recipe data (such as ingredients and cooking steps) locally inside your browser to display the Focus Reader modal.

2. No Browsing History Collection

  • SimpleBrowsing never collects, tracks, sells, or transmits your browsing history, page URLs, or personal reading activity to external servers.
  • There are no third-party analytics scripts, advertising trackers, or background data harvesters in the extension.

3. Local Preference Storage

Your visual and audio comfort settings—such as Dark Slate or Soft Light mode, custom text colours, text size, Dyslexia Spacing, Reading Spotlight, Read Aloud voice/speed/volume, and Pro unlock status—are stored locally on your device using chrome.storage.local.

4. Optional External Links & Affiliate Disclosure

On recipe pages, SimpleBrowsing offers optional Shop Ingredients and Kitchen Tools buttons. Clicking one of these links opens your local Amazon storefront (such as amazon.co.uk, amazon.pl, amazon.de, or amazon.com) in a new tab.

As an Amazon Associate, I earn from qualifying purchases. These links may include an affiliate partner tag so SimpleBrowsing can earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. No personal data from SimpleBrowsing is sent to Amazon when you browse recipes; Amazon's own privacy policy applies only if you choose to click an external link and visit their site.

5. SimpleBrowsing Pro Licence Verification

If you choose to purchase the optional £3 one-time SimpleBrowsing Pro upgrade, your licence key is verified to unlock Pro features (Custom Themes, Recipe Scaling, and Unit Conversion) and stored locally in chrome.storage.local so you do not need to re-enter it.

6. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or need help using SimpleBrowsing, please contact support@simplebrowsing.app.
